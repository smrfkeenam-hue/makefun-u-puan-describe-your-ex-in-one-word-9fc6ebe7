# Describe Your Ex in One Word — agent notes

Competition: u-puan-describe-your-ex-in-one-word-9fc6ebe7 (program/v1).
The entry is exactly one word, at most 111 characters, written by `main.py`
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.

Chosen word: **Unsubscribe**.

Why: the judge weighs whether the word reads instantly as a verdict on an ex,
then comic punch, surprise, craft and recognition. "Unsubscribe" frames the
whole relationship as a newsletter nobody signed up for — the joke lands at a
glance, it is not a plain insult, it is spelled cleanly as one word, and
anyone who has ever opted out of unwanted mail recognises the feeling at once.

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library (`import os`) and writes the
entry from a single constant.
